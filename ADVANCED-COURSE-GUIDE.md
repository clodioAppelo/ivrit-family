# Ivrit Family — A0–C2 advanced expansion

This release retains the existing family profiles and practice tools and adds 16 C1 and 16 C2 guided advanced units (32 new units), with Hebrew examples, English and Spanish meaning, listening playback, printed and cursive displays, comprehension checks, and open-ended production prompts.

**Scope limitation:** This is a substantial self-study expansion, not a full accredited A0–C2 curriculum. The C1/C2 lessons are introductory advanced-language modules; speech recognition and writing evaluation are not equivalent to teacher assessment. Completion indicates practice, not CEFR certification.

## Updating GitHub Pages

Upload the extracted contents into the root of the existing `ivrit-family` repository, replacing `index.html`, `sw.js`, and other matching files. Commit changes and wait for Pages to deploy. On iPhones, reload the site and relaunch the installed Home Screen app. Avoid clearing site data if you want to retain local progress.

## Testing

1. Switch between Maria, Adriel, and your profile; confirm language and progress remain separate.
2. Open Course, choose C1 then C2, and check several units.
3. Test Hebrew audio and print/cursive display on iPhone Safari.
4. Confirm the existing Alphabet, Gender, Handwriting, and Skill Studio tabs still work.
5. Confirm installed PWA refreshes to the new release.
