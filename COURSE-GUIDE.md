# Ivrit Family — structured course edition

This edition contains **44 original bilingual grammar/skill modules**, plus the existing **120 phrase-based practice lessons**, the alphabet, handwriting, and gender lessons. These are different kinds of content and should not be described as 164 fully graded classroom lessons.

## Learning path
A0: alphabet, sounds and foundations. A1: everyday grammar and communication. A2: tenses and practical situations. B1: connected speech and explanations. B2: advanced discussion prompts. The B1/B2 material is introductory practice, not a comprehensive CEFR-certified B2 course.

Each module includes an explanation in English and Spanish, a print/handwriting example, audio where supported, a speaking task, a comprehension check, and locally saved completion. Complete modules in order, then use Conversation, Gender, Alphabet and Handwriting for reinforcement.

## Updating GitHub Pages
Upload **the contents** of this folder (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icons/) to the root of your existing `ivrit-family` repository. Do not upload the ZIP as the site. Commit, wait for Pages deployment, then close and reopen the installed iPhone app. If old content persists, remove/re-add the Home Screen app or clear website data after checking progress.

## Limitations
Progress is stored locally per device/profile; there is no account synchronization. Browser speech recognition is not a dependable phonetic assessment. Hebrew audio depends on installed voices. The cursive font is loaded online. This is a substantial self-study foundation, not a guarantee of fluency.
